# Electricity examples

These cards show total and per-phase electricity measurements with different horseshoe shapes, directions, scales, labels, and color treatments.

![Electricity cards](../assets/screenshots/fhs-demo-card-23-electricity--dark.webp){width="300"}

## :material-horseshoe: What these examples demonstrate

- full and partial horseshoe arcs;
- clockwise and counterclockwise values;
- major and minor tick marks;
- labels and color stops;
- groups for repeated phase layouts;
- shared geometry with `same_as`, `ref()`, and `calc()`;
- external light and dark palettes.

Replace the DSMR entity IDs with the sensors used by your installation.
