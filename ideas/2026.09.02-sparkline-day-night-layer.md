# Sparkline Day/Night Layer

## Goal

Show exact day and night periods from Home Assistant's `sun.sun` entity behind
historical sparkline data. Sunrise and sunset remain tied to time rather than
to graph bins.

The layer can fill the complete plot or become a separate cartesian strip or
radial ring. It is shared by every visible series and never becomes a graph
series itself.

## Configuration

```yaml
sparkline:
  show:
    day_night: true

  day_night:
    mode: band
    position: bottom
    size: 4
    offset: 0

    day:
      styles:
        - fill: transparent

    night:
      styles:
        - fill: var(--divider-color)
        - fill-opacity: 0.2
```

`background` fills the cartesian draw area or radial graph ring. `band` draws
a strip at the top or bottom of a cartesian draw area. On radial charts it
draws a concentric ring directly inside the graph. A negative offset moves the
band farther inward and a positive offset moves it outward, without reserving
graph or axis space.

The source is always `sun.sun`. The old experimental
`radial_barcode.face.show_day_night` circle is removed.

## Architecture

- `SparklineGraphTool` owns the `sun.sun` history request, current state,
  forecast transitions and SVG rendering.
- `SparklineGraph` maps exact time ranges to cartesian rectangles or radial
  sectors using its authoritative draw area, arc and rotation.
- `SparklineSeries` remains unchanged. Series offsets do not create separate
  day/night layers.
- The parent sparkline period defines the visible day/night timeline.

Historical transitions supply completed periods. An active calendar day uses
history up to now and `next_rising` and `next_setting` for the remainder of the
day. Sun history is cached independently and never activates the graph loading
spinner.

## Constraints

- Supported for historical cartesian charts, radial and radial barcode.
- Not supported for `real_time`.
- Future calendar offsets are outside scope.
- Day/night renders behind graph data, grid, axes, tickmarks and labels.
- Both states have independent normal FHS style maps.

## Tests

- Exact transitions remain independent of bin density.
- Active calendar history and future transitions form one continuous day/night
  timeline.
- Historical calendar and rolling windows use only their represented range.
- Multiple series share one layer.
- Cartesian background, top band and bottom band use the same time segments.
- Radial background and inner band follow arc, rotation, size and offset.
- Full circles, partial arcs and the zero-degree boundary produce valid paths.
- Disabled day/night makes no extra request and renders no layer.
