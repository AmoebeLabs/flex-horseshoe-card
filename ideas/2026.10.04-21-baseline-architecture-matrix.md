## Inventory Boundary

This matrix identifies ownership before production edits. Measurement signatures
protect derived estimates and overflow calculations; they do not imply that every
`updated()` call avoids DOM measurements. Effective-style setters remain part of
the tool contract wherever style changes invalidate measurement. Final cache
retention decisions require the focused tests and fixed benchmark evidence.
